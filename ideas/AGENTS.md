# Ideation rules

- Treat every idea in this subtree as a hypothesis, regardless of confidence or polish.
- Start with a specific user and observed problem. Separate problem evidence from the proposed solution.
- Challenge assumptions, research current alternatives, and phrase differentiation as falsifiable claims.
- Do not inflate gate scores or hide disconfirming evidence. Mark evidence, inference, and unknowns.
- Define the cheapest experiment that could reject the idea and a demo moment that proves behavior.
- Preserve why rejected/superseded ideas failed when moving them to `archive/`.
- Never update canonical product or architecture as selected unless the team explicitly instructs it.
