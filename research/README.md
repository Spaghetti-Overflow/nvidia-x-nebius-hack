# Research contract

`research/` stores external evidence and analysis. It is not accepted product truth.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer official documentation, repositories, engineering posts, and creator papers.
- Put `Last checked: YYYY-MM-DD` on time-sensitive notes.
- Link important claims to their source. Never infer private internals from product behavior.
- Mark **Fact**, **Inference**, **Hypothesis**, and **Unknown** when the boundary could be confused.
- Compare systems by architectural responsibility and trade-off, not feature count.
- Keep notes compact enough to scan; link to source detail instead of copying it.

Research becomes accepted only when the team updates the appropriate file in [`../docs/`](../docs/README.md), normally with a decision record for material choices. Preserve contrary evidence and unresolved uncertainty.
